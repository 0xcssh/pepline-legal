Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

In short: your journal stays on your iPhone. Pepline has no account system of its own, sells no data, shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

1. What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter — compounds, vials, doses, injection sites, schedules, notes, weight, side effects, food and water, and progress photos — is stored only in Pepline's private storage on your device, protected by iOS data protection. It is not uploaded to a server and is not stored in iCloud. Deleting the app deletes it; Settings → Delete my account can also erase it.

2. Anonymous usage analytics

Pepline sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck (for example "a dose was logged" or "the paywall was shown") to understand which features are used. They contain counts and categories only — never names, doses, dates, notes or health values — and are not linked to your identity.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Pepline uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; RevenueCat receives a random identifier and your App Store purchase receipts, not your journal. Pepline never sees your payment details.

4. Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Pepline keeps Apple's anonymous user identifier in your iPhone's keychain and uses it to tie Pepline Pro to your Apple ID with RevenueCat. Settings → Delete my account revokes Pepline's access at Apple through our server and forgets the identifier.

5. Meal photo estimates (optional, Pepline Pro)

Only after you allow it on the consent screen, a meal photo you take is sent over an encrypted connection to Pepline's server and to Anthropic (Claude), a third-party AI service, solely to estimate the foods and their nutrients. The request carries the photo, your RevenueCat identifier (to check your subscription) and your app language — nothing from your journal. Pepline does not keep the photo; Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and privacy policy. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

6. Food search and barcodes

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or the barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts, an open food database, to look the product up. No other data is sent.

7. Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on, Pepline reads and writes your body weight in Apple Health. Health data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to third parties and is never used for advertising.

8. Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used for progress photos, meal photos and barcodes. Progress photos are stored on your device only. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

9. Children

Pepline is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

10. Your rights

Because your journal lives on your device, you control it directly: export it (CSV, PDF) or delete it at any time. For any question about your data or to exercise your rights under applicable law (such as the GDPR), write to us.
